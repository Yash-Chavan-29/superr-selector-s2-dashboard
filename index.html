Why Jasprit Bumrah sits at #11—and why we didn't hide it

Every casual fan puts Jasprit Bumrah #1 by reputation. In our audited 2022–2026 data, Bumrah is extraordinary everywhere—posting +24.08 runs/100 balls in ordinary overs and +24.04 under peak pressure (+1.87 runs saved per over across 52 spells).

Because his normal baseline is already at the ceiling, his pressure delta is flat (−0.04), placing him 0.9 PRR points behind #10 Kuldeep Yadav (71.2). Keeping him transparently at #11 proves our rankings follow the data—not reputation—while surfacing true pressure risers like Varun Chakravarthy (+13.9 Delta), Adam Zampa (+23.6 Delta), and Shashank Singh (+14.1 Delta).
